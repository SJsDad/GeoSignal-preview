# v0.10 validation and limits

Nominal fitting errors are 0.018 / 0.021 nm for the two presets. These are model residuals, not process prediction accuracy. Process calibration uses measured CDs across patterns and focus/exposure conditions. [Mack et al.](https://www.lithoguru.com/scientist/litho_papers/2007_156_Improved%20Methods%20for%20Lithography%20Model%20Calibration.pdf)

| Comparison | Max CD/space delta (nm) | Max edge delta (nm) |
|---|---:|---:|
| dense7_32 vs dense11_32 | 6.495 | 3.660 |
| dense11_50 vs dense11_32 | 4.166 | 2.184 |
| dense11_64 vs dense11_32 | 2.812 | 1.937 |

Comparisons fix anchor, pixel, optics and dose convention. Ambit comparisons freeze the dense11/.32 Tnorm. Neither monotonic nor 1 nm convergence is established; .32 µm remains a cost-conscious study default. After stable raster-boundary handling, dense7/11/13 each have six ROI components and three central-strip components in the inspected condition. Counts do not establish global topology equivalence.
