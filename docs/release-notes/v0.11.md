# v0.11 — Optical Sampling & Numerical Accuracy Benchmark

**v0.11.0 releases a reusable benchmark and analysis framework.** It evaluates numerical error and cost while retaining GeoSignal's KrF scalar Abbe model. Render integration of the new sampling options is deferred for a separate review. The public demo retains the [v0.10]({{ '/release-notes/v0.10/' | relative_url }}) Speed/Internal options, defaults and screen.

<h2 id="conclusions">1. Conclusions and candidates</h2>

Exact Area and Cubic B-Spline are useful candidates, but source bias and initial mask preparation must be considered separately. Dense7 was insufficient for the study's quantitative CD criteria. The proposed normal candidate uses Cell-area51/Mask10/AI25; the precise candidate uses Cell-area51/Mask5/AI25. These are research labels, not deployed UI options or guarantees for arbitrary layouts.

| Candidate | Dense | Weighting / points | Mask nm | Raster | Native AI nm | Interpolation | Worst CD error nm | Worst AI RMSE |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Existing comparison | 7 | Equal / 29 | 10 | Binary center | 10 | Bilinear | 56.2730 | 0.0525000 |
| Fast preview | 7 | Equal / 29 | 25 | Exact Area | 25 | Cubic B-Spline | 15.3940 | 0.0147937 |

| Precise | 51 | Cell-area / 2077 | 5 | Exact Area | 25 | Cubic B-Spline | 0.4403 | 0.000721803 |

Accuracy is the maximum over the selected **periodic L/S and contact cases, nominal focus, raw T0=0.30/D0=1**, against a continuous-source reference. Existing/preview use shared mask/image phase; normal/precise include independent phases. Missing reference crossings remain explicit statuses rather than zero CD error; the existing condition also has false-printing cases. CD alone is insufficient to assess quality.

Normal targets CD≤2nm/RMSE≤0.005; precise targets CD≤0.5nm/RMSE≤0.005. Preview does not meet these quantitative gates. These are not TT04 or wafer-accuracy figures. TT04 runtime has a [separate scope](#render).

<h2 id="reference">3. Reference and numerical validation</h2>

For a centered periodic binary line, `c_m=(W/P)sinc(mW/P)`. At source `(sx,sy)`, only orders satisfying `(m/P−sx)²+sy²≤(NA/λ)²` enter the field sum. This avoids mask rasterization and unnecessary2D FFTs.

At nominal focus, continuous uniform-source order-pair weights are areas shared by the source disk and two shifted pupil disks. Adaptive slice integration uses circle intersections as breakpoints, with independent quadrature/high-density checks. Dense201 and the smallest pixel are not assumed exact.

| Direct comparison | Numerical stage |
|---|---|
| R0 | Exact geometry Fourier coefficients and continuous source |
| R1↔R0 | Discrete-source integration |
| R2↔R1 | Raster/DFT representation with the same discrete source |
| R3↔R2 | Interpolation of the same raw AI samples |
| R3↔R0 | End-to-end error |

Stage errors are not added or subtracted as causal contributions. The reference validates the scalar model numerically, not measured optics/resist behavior. Tightening the initial1D integration tolerance changed AI by5.55e−17; integration error estimates are not universal bounds.

The initial cross-check failed four noncommensurate pitch/pixel cases. Restricting the reduced calculation to original grating orders missed optical-passband supercell frequencies introduced by raster aliasing. The **benchmark reduction** was corrected to retain all relevant DFT components, and160blocks/20,480rows were recomputed. Failed evidence and migration records remain preserved. Production physics did not change.

Final342 real2D Abbe checks had maximum residual9.80e−13 against a1e−10 tolerance. Native binary mask comparison found zero mismatches in216cases. Normal's extra2D checks had residuals1.09e−14 for L/S and9.60e−15 for contact.

<h2 id="source">4. Conventional source convergence</h2>

Dense is a Cartesian grid-size label, not the actual circular-source point count. Equal weights apply to nodes inside the disk. **Cell-area is quadrature for the same uniform disk**, including boundary-cell areas and representative-node handling. Boundary nodes can be radially projected, and counts differ. It is neither measured scanner illumination nor a weighting-only comparison on identical nodes.

<h2 id="raster">5. Mask rasterization</h2>

Binary sampling reuses the production center/boundary rule. Exact Area computes pixel–polygon intersection area divided by pixel area: an average amplitude representation of binary geometry, not a physical gray mask. Tests cover holes, subpixel translation, periodic boundaries and area conservation.

SSAA2/4/8 approximates coverage with averaged binary subpixels and need not converge monotonically at every finite factor. Identical-sample MSAA and box/area averaging were not duplicated. Gaussian smoothing was not presumed beneficial. A bandlimited oracle permits negative/above-one mask values for diagnosis and is excluded from deployment candidates. FXAA was excluded.

Optical-passband spectrum and R2↔R1 AI/CD matter more than mask appearance. Exact Area substantially reduces binary quantization and phase errors; area preservation alone does not establish Fourier/CD accuracy. Area25nm does not replace the precise configuration.

![Raster-stage error versus pixel]({{ '/assets/release-notes/v0.11/02_full_raster_stage.png' | relative_url }})

General-polygon Exact Area currently visits every cell. Its initial preparation is much slower than specialized periodic interval coverage. Boundary-cell optimization and mask/FFT caching are integration priorities.

<h2 id="interpolation">6. Raw-intensity interpolation</h2>

All methods receive identical raw AI samples before thresholding. Keys cubic(`a=−0.5`) differs from prefiltered Cubic B-Spline. Finite sinc weights are normalized; Blackman radii2/4/8 are tested. Periodic extension is explicit. Negative intensity, overshoot and ringing are retained as diagnostics.

Independent-phase Mask5/AI25/Cell-area51 results:

| Method | Worst total CD nm | Worst interpolation-only CD nm | Precise total/stage gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Nearest | 22.9658 | 23.1144 | Fail |
| Bilinear | 7.8710 | 7.7809 | Fail |
| Keys Bicubic | 0.6732 | 0.2383 | Fail |
| Cubic B-Spline | 0.4403 | 0.0324 | Pass |
| Truncated SincR4 | 27.2586 | 27.0408 | Fail |
| BlackmanR2 | 6.4867 | 6.4719 | Fail |
| BlackmanR4 | 0.5097 | 0.2409 | Fail |
| BlackmanR8 | 0.4393 | 0.0985 | Pass |

BlackmanR8's total error is about0.001nm smaller, but B-Spline has lower conditional interpolation error. Cancellation with other stages does not establish interpolation superiority. Larger radii and the sinc label do not guarantee improvement, and interpolation cannot recover lost aliased information.

<h2 id="split-grid">7. Fine mask / coarse image grid</h2>

Production still shares mask and native AI grids. The benchmark adapter moves **fine-mask FFT passband coefficients into a smaller field FFT over the same physical domain**. It corrects2D normalization `(Nout/Nmask)²` and pixel-center origin phase and checks `Nyquist_AI>max(NA/λ+source_radius,2NA/λ)`. Frequency spacing and transmitted fields are retained before calling unchanged production Abbe computation.

| Source / mask·AI nm / B-Spline | Worst CD nm | Worst RMSE | L/S optics s | Contact optics s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cell-area51 / 5·5 | 0.4350 | 0.000712 | 18.1387 | 3.1284 |
| Cell-area51 / 5·25 | 0.4403 | 0.000722 | 0.5256 | 0.1093 |
| Cell-area51 / 10·25, normal | 1.3416 | 0.001095 | 0.5208 | 0.1160 |
| Equal101 / 5·25 | 0.4593 | 0.000280 | 1.9451 | 0.4298 |

Times are real2D Abbe median3 warm calls on Cloud, L/S3µm/contact1.2µm, excluding mask preparation and export. Shared and independent rows have different phase scopes; small accuracy differences do not establish superiority. Optical computation alone improves about34.5×/28.6× for the same fine mask/source. **These are not HTTP speedups.**

<h2 id="cross">8. Grid phase and controlled comparisons</h2>

Mask/image phases0/.25/.5/.75 are aligned in physical coordinates. Contact evaluates16mask XY phases ×16image XY phases=256pairs. Benchmark-only0.025nm geometry precision represents quarter phases exactly; production0.1nm DBU is retained.

![Grid-phase sensitivity]({{ '/assets/release-notes/v0.11/05_full_grid_phase.png' | relative_url }})

The full study includes3,114rows passing the total gate while failing a stage gate. LS137/Dense21/Area20 has total CD error0.0856nm but source/raster conditional errors1.5660/1.4782nm. Small final error can reflect cancellation.

![Conditional-error cancellation]({{ '/assets/release-notes/v0.11/06_error_cancellation.png' | relative_url }})

The tables provide28,032configuration summaries,115,920pairs holding other variables/phase sets fixed and300variable-effect rows. Configuration standard deviation is not repeated-measurement uncertainty. Only295canonical rows have real timing matches for all physical settings and phases; other timings remain missing. Fourier reduction,2D FFT, polygon preparation and whole HTTP have separate scopes. Pareto selection requires a case, valid crossings, stage gates and comparable measured tasks; no universal optimum is claimed.

![Raster/interpolation combinations]({{ '/assets/release-notes/v0.11/04_full_cross_heatmap.png' | relative_url }})

The recommended contact interpolator was checked across all256phase pairs at10/5/2/1nm evaluation meshes. AI25 worst contact RMSE changed from7.98378e−5 to7.98340e−5 between10nm and1nm evaluation. CD uses separate threshold-crossing root searches.

<h2 id="render">9. TT04PWM and Render feasibility</h2>

Both67/20 and68/20 exist in the public TT04 fixture. The registered67/20 reference is170nm at(147.770,108.460)µm/y. A geometry-validated140nm transect at(129.490,91.3125)µm/x was selected for68/20 resource measurement only; it was not registered as a new product reference. The current UI requires a valid Custom Anchor for68/20.

`render.yaml` selects Free, one Gunicorn worker and300s timeout. [Official Free specs](https://render.com/docs/compute-plans) are0.1CPU/512MB. These measurements use a **comparable0.1CPU/512MiB/no-swap Docker limit**, not the actual hosted service or authenticated Dashboard.

| Candidate | 67/20 one ROI s | 68/20 one ROI s | Product/Free assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Existing | 2.125 | 1.427 | Existing path; Custom Anchor for68 |
| Preview | 3.000 | 2.021 | Candidate after Area/B-Spline integration |
| Normal | 19.118 | 18.191 | Integrate, then validate nominal focus/few ROIs |
| Precise | 51.107 | 49.101 | Separate640mask-axis limit; cache/few ROIs |

This includes GDS read/clip, raster, FFT, source, nominal Abbe and gauge over the same3.2µm domain. Sources are reused after first construction. Imports, DRC selection, D0 calibration, contours/plots/CSV/GDS/HTML and network are excluded. It is not service TAT. Quota timings are single calls; short wall-time stages vary with CPU quota bursts. Native optical calls use median3.

Preview's initial TT04 calculation was about1.4× slower than existing because Exact Area preparation dominates. Cached optical computation is about8–9× faster. Normal and precise share source/image grids and similar repeated optical cost; preparation differs. The adapter's cumulative quota RSS peak was145.1MiB.

The **whole existing Speed Gunicorn HTTP request passed under the same quota in119.899s, with350.16MiB container peak memory**. The reference CSV gave170.018074nm and D0.82421875. Fitting residual is not optical accuracy. Container peak includes master/worker/client and charged page cache, unlike worker RSS.

An initial HTTP test falsely failed because it expected an internal ID in visible HTML. The correct result CSV was verified; the test now checks exported nominal CD/D0 and passed on rerun. Failed evidence is documented.

The default focus/dose sweep is **off**. Enabling it gives17focus×21dose, without repeating FFT for each dose. Extrapolating measured−0.2/+0.2µm CPU cost to6ROI×17focus at0.1CPU gives roughly22–30minutes of normal/precise optics alone. This is a scale estimate, not a measured full sweep. It exceeds the300s synchronous request budget. New candidates' complete HTTP and actual Render deployment remain untested.

<h2 id="validation">10. Tests and reproduction</h2>

- Full regression: **192 passed**. Separate resource-audit tests:40passed; existing whole HTTP smoke passed.
- Tests cover mask area/holes/SSAA/translation/periodicity, constant/sine/Nyquist/sample preservation/kernel normalization/boundaries/ringing, analytical-vs-FFT/raw scale/crossings/CD.
- Small24²-grid full-rank SOCS agrees with identical-discrete-source Abbe to4.44e−15. Positive mode count need not equal source-point count; truncation error is evaluated separately from source integration.
- Commercial1024×1024 TCC storage is not reproduced, and `max_frequency_count=1024` is not treated as that grid or kernel count.

<h2 id="limits">11. Limits and integration priorities</h2>

This is a selected-periodic-pattern, one-contact, nominal-focus scalar-model study. Measured scanner distributions, vector/mask3D/resist/PEB/development, wafer calibration and general contour EPE are outside scope. TT04 resource feasibility is not TT04 optical-accuracy convergence. Smaller pixels, denser sources and longer kernels are not inherently better.

Review Render screen/options separately. Add opt-in Area/B-Spline with consistent raw-intensity gauge/contour coordinates and boundaries, optimize boundary cells and cache source/mask/FFT data, then validate normal Mask10/AI25 on nominal/few-ROI requests. Precise Mask5 needs an independent mask cap/memory budget. Do not silently reduce ROI/ambit for comparisons. Full sweeps need separate compute/asynchronous E2E validation.

Lab is preserved on `release/v0.11.0`/`v0.11.0` while Render-connected `main` remains unchanged; only Pages publishes these notes.
